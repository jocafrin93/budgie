What to Look For

Key things to check in the console output:
1. Paycheck Data: Are your paychecks configured correctly with accountDistribution?
2. Account IDs: Do the account IDs in paychecks match the account IDs in categories?
3. Date Filtering: Are paychecks being filtered correctly by account?
4. Date Comparison: Are the date comparisons working correctly?
5. Final Result: What's the actual result of the calculation?

Common Issues to Check

Issue What to Look For Solution
Account ID Mismatch Account IDs in paychecks don't match category account IDs Update paycheck or category account assignments
No Account Distribution Paychecks missing accountDistribution arrays Re-configure paychecks to include account distribution
Date Format Issues Dates not parsing correctly Check date format consistency
No Paychecks Generated Empty upcoming paychecks array Check paycheck start dates and frequency settings
Wrong Date Comparison All paychecks filtered out by date comparison Check if due date is too soon or paycheck dates are wrong
